# 📊 Excel Data Analysis & Dashboard

## 1. Overview

Excel was used to analyze **product sales, regional performance, returns, and delivery data**.

---

## 2. Analysis Performed

* Calculated **Total Revenue, Total Orders, Quantity Sold, and Return Rate**.
* Analyzed **product-wise sales and performance**.
* Compared **sales and revenue across regions**.
* Analyzed **delivery time and shipping costs**.
* Used Excel formulas such as `SUM`, `COUNTIF`, `SUMIF`, `AVERAGE`, and `AVERAGEIF`.
