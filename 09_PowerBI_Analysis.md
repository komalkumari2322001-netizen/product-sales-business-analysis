# 📊 Power BI Analysis

## 📌 Project

**Product Sales & Business Performance Analysis**

## 🎯 Dashboard Purpose

An interactive Power BI dashboard to understand sales, products, regions, returns, promotions, shipping costs, and delivery performance.

## 📄 Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Sales & Product Analysis

- **KPI Cards:** Total Revenue, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Return Rate, Total Shipping Cost, Average Delivery Days
- **Revenue by Product:** Column Chart
- **Revenue Trend:** Line Chart by Year, Quarter and Month
- **Quantity by Product:** Pie Chart
- **Return Rate by Product:** Bar Chart
- **Filters:** Region, Customer Type, Promotion, Product, Year

### Page 2 — Regional & Business Analysis

- **Revenue by Region:** Column Chart
- **Orders by Region:** Pie Chart
- **Revenue by Promotion:** Column Chart
- **Average Discount by Customer Type:** Column Chart
- **Average Shipping Cost by Region:** Bar Chart
- **Average Delivery Days by Region:** Donut Chart

## 📌 Dashboard Summary

The dashboard gives a quick view of:

- **Revenue:** 4.38M
- **Orders:** 1.5K
- **Average Order Value:** 2.92K
- **Return Rate:** 24.8%
- **Shipping Cost:** 41.26K
- **Average Delivery:** 6.04 days

### Key Findings

- **Tablet** has the highest product revenue.
- **Chair** has the highest return rate.
- **North** has the highest regional revenue.
- **FREESHIP** has the highest promotion revenue.
- **South** has the highest average shipping cost.
- **North** has the highest average delivery time.
