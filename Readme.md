# 📊 Product Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end **Business Analysis project** focused on analyzing sales, product returns, regional performance, discounts, shipping costs, and delivery time.

The goal is to identify business issues and provide **data-driven recommendations** to improve revenue, reduce costs, and improve customer experience.

## 🎯 Business Problems

* High return rates for certain products
* Revenue differences across regions
* Impact of discounts and promotions on sales
* Shipping cost inefficiencies
* Delays in order delivery

## 🔍 Key Business Problems

1. Product Return Leakage

Return rates are high for some product categories, especially Chairs and Laptops, which may increase revenue loss and operational costs.

2. Regional Revenue Disparity

Sales performance differs across regions, with some regions generating significantly more revenue than others.

3. Discount & Promotion Impact

Promotional codes and discounts may reduce revenue and margins without clearly increasing transaction value.

4. Shipping Cost Optimization

Shipping costs vary across orders and may have a greater impact on smaller-value orders.

5. Delivery Performance

The average order-to-delivery time is around 6 days, which may affect customer satisfaction and increase cancellation risk.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze sales and revenue performance
* Analyze product return performance and identify return-prone products
* Compare regional sales performance
* Evaluate discounts and promotions
* Analyze shipping costs and delivery time
* Provide business recommendations
