# 📊 SQL Analysis

## 📌 Project

**Product Sales & Business Performance Analysis**

## 🎯 Project Purpose

SQL is used to analyze product sales data and identify business problems related to revenue, product returns, regional performance, discounts, shipping costs, and delivery time.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze total sales and revenue.
- Calculate total orders and average order value.
- Identify products with high return rates.
- Compare revenue and orders across regions.
- Evaluate discounts and promotions.
- Analyze shipping costs and delivery performance.
- Support business recommendations using SQL insights.

## 🔍 SQL Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Analysis

- Calculate total revenue.
- Calculate total orders.
- Calculate average order value.
- Analyze revenue by product and region.
- Compare quantity sold across products.

### 2. Product Return Analysis

- Calculate returned orders by product.
- Calculate return rates by product.
- Identify products with high return rates for further investigation.

### 3. Regional Analysis

- Compare revenue across regions.
- Analyze order volume by region.
- Compare regional shipping costs.
- Analyze average delivery time by region.

### 4. Discount and Promotion Analysis

- Calculate average discount by customer type.
- Analyze revenue generated through promotions.
- Support evaluation of discount and promotion performance.

### 5. Shipping and Delivery Analysis

- Calculate total shipping costs.
- Calculate average shipping costs by region.
- Analyze average delivery time across regions.
