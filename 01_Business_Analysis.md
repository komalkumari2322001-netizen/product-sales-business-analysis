
# 📊 Business Analysis

## 1. Business Problem

The business is facing challenges related to product returns, regional sales, discounts, shipping costs, and delivery delays.

### Key Problems
- High return rates for certain products.
- Revenue differences across regions.
- Discount and promotion impact on sales.
- Shipping cost inefficiencies.
- Delays in order delivery.

### Business Objective
To analyze sales data and provide recommendations to improve revenue, reduce costs, and improve customer experience.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Management | Improve revenue and reduce costs |
| Sales Manager | Analyze regional and product sales |
| Operations Manager | Reduce shipping costs and delivery delays |
| Product Manager | Analyze product returns |
| Marketing Manager | Evaluate discounts and promotions |
| Finance Manager | Monitor revenue and costs |

---

## 3. Gap Analysis

| Business Area | Identified Gap | Improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Product Returns | High return rates | Improve product quality |
| Regional Sales | Uneven sales performance | Improve regional sales |
| Discounts | Possible margin reduction | Review discount effectiveness |
| Shipping | Cost inefficiency | Optimize shipping costs |
| Delivery | Potential delays | Improve delivery performance |

---

## Expected Outcome

- Reduce product return rates.
- Improve regional sales performance.
- Optimize discounts and promotions.
- Control shipping costs.
- Improve delivery performance.
