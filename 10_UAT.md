# 🧪 User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

## 1. Overview

UAT was performed to verify that the **Product Sales & Business Performance Analysis** meets the defined business requirements.

---

## 2. UAT Objectives

The main objectives of UAT were:

- Verify that business requirements are covered.
- Check the accuracy of KPIs and calculations.
- Validate product returns and return rates.
- Verify regional sales and revenue analysis.
- Check discount and promotion analysis.
- Validate shipping cost and delivery time.
- Ensure the Power BI dashboard is easy to understand and use.
