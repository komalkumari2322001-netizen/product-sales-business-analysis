# 💡 Business Recommendations

## 1. Overview

Based on the analysis of sales, returns, regional performance, promotions, shipping costs, and delivery time, the following recommendations are proposed.

---

## 2. Key Recommendations

### 📦 Product Returns
**Chair** has the highest return rate.

- Investigate the main reasons for Chair returns.
- Review product quality, specifications, and customer expectations.
- Monitor return rate regularly to reduce revenue leakage.

### 💰 Product Sales
**Tablet** generates the highest product revenue.

- Maintain product availability and inventory levels.
- Focus on promoting high-performing products.
- Analyze what factors are contributing to strong Tablet sales.

### 🌍 Regional Performance
**North** generates the highest regional revenue.

- Use North as a benchmark for other regions.
- Identify practices contributing to its strong sales performance.
- Focus improvement efforts on lower-performing regions.

### 🎟️ Promotions
**FREESHIP** generates the highest promotion revenue.

- Continue monitoring FREESHIP performance.
- Compare promotional revenue with discount and shipping costs.
- Use promotion performance to support future campaign decisions.

### 🚚 Shipping Cost
**South** has the highest average shipping cost.

- Review shipping methods and delivery routes in South.
- Identify opportunities to reduce shipping expenses.
- Compare South's shipping cost with other regions.

### ⏱️ Delivery Performance
**North** has the highest average delivery time.

- Investigate the reasons for longer delivery times in North.
- Review order processing and courier performance.
- Improve delivery coordination to reduce delivery time.

---

## 3. Expected Business Impact

These recommendations can help the business:

- Reduce product return rates.
- Maintain strong product revenue.
- Improve lower-performing regions.
- Optimize promotional spending.
- Reduce shipping costs.
- Improve delivery performance.

---

## 4. Conclusion

The recommendations are based on the actual findings from the **Excel, SQL, and Power BI analysis** and provide clear areas for business and operational improvement.
